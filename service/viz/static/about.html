Columnist

Columnist is a foundation model for osu!mania 4K charts. It is pretrained on unlabelled charts and then adapted to specific tasks: pattern classification, per-note pattern readout (the player page), and chart similarity (the similar-charts page).

Downloads

Models: Hugging Face (models) · Data: Hugging Face (dataset) · Code: GitHub

What the data is

The released data is chart ids, labels and sources. No .osu files, replays or audio are redistributed. Charts remain the property of their mappers. Use of osu! data complies with fair research use.

Limits

4K only. No snap or BPM-grid information is used. Long-note patterns are the weakest area. This is a research tool and not an official osu! tool; it must not be used to make Beatmap Nominator decisions on its own.
