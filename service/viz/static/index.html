Watch the patterns as the chart plays

Drop an .osz (charts and audio) or a single .osu here, or paste a beatmapset link above. Links download the set from a public mirror when one answers, and fall back to the charts alone (no audio) from osu.ppy.sh.

Every note gets its own score, and the bars follow the notes you have just played (the last 2 s, fading out after a section ends), so nothing fires before it reaches the hit line. Bars light up past the chart-level cut (white tick) and get a dot past the strong cut (coloured tick), the section score that best tells labelled charts apart.
